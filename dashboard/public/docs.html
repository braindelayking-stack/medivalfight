🎮 Getting Started

  • Use /profile create to create your character and get 100 free points to distribute!
  • Use /profile view to see your current stats!
  • Use /profile distribute to distribute your free points!
  • Use /boss fight to battle bosses and earn coins!
  • Use /abilities buy to buy abilities!
  • Use /abilities upgrade to upgrade your abilities (up to 15 levels!)!

💪 Stats Explained

  • ⚔️ Strength: Increases your damage in battle, used for Strength ability upgrades!
  • 💰 Wealth: Increases your coin earnings, used for Wealth ability upgrades!
  • 🏃 Agility: Higher chance to dodge attacks, used for Agility ability upgrades!
  • 🧘 Composure/Health: Increases max HP (base HP is 25, +5 HP per point)! Used for Composure ability upgrades!

📜 Abilities

🟢 Weak Abilities (20 coins each)

🔥 Fire Attack

Shoot a fireball dealing 10 base damage

💧 Water Attack

Shoot a waterball dealing 10 base damage

🌍 Earth Attack

Shoot an earthball dealing 10 base damage

💨 Air Attack

Shoot an airball dealing 10 base damage

💰 Sacrifice

Spend coins to heal yourself during battle

🏃 Dodge

Dodge the next attack with 50% chance

⚡ Attack Boost

Boost your next attack by 2x

🧘 Composure Defense

Reduce damage by 50% when below 20% HP

🟡 Mid-Tier Abilities

💥 Smash (100 strength)

30 base damage, shatters boss shields, but you take 1.2x damage next hit

💵 Bribe (120 wealth)

Spend 50 wealth to skip the boss's next attack

🏃‍♂️ Quick Step (110 agility)

Dodge the next boss attack and counter for 15 damage

🛡️ Iron Will (90 composure)

Reduce all incoming damage by 50% for 5 seconds

🔴 Strong Abilities

-

🔥 Berserk (250 strength)

Sacrifice 20% current HP to deal 50 base damage

✨ Golden Touch (280 wealth)

Convert 30 wealth into 30 points AND deal 25 damage

👤 Shadow Strike (260 agility)

40 damage and stun the boss for 2 seconds

⚔️ Last Stand (270 composure)

If below 20% HP: heal to 50% max HP and 2x attack boost (once per fight)

⬆️ Ability Upgrades

  • Use /abilities upgrade view to see upgrade levels for your abilities!
  • Use /abilities upgrade to spend attribute points (strength/wealth/agility/composure) to upgrade abilities!
  • Each upgrade costs (30 + current level * 30) points of the corresponding attribute!
  • Max upgrade level is 15!
