🎮 How to Play

⚔️ Turn-Based Fights

Battle against bosses in tactical turn-based fights! Utilize your abilities, dodge attacks, and score critical hits for massive rewards!

⬆️ Ability Leveling

All of your abilities can be leveled up to 15 with your attribute points! The higher the level, the more damage, healing, and effect you get!

📈 Attribute Details

  • Strength: Gives you a boost in direct damage dealt – perfect for abilities such as Berserk and Smash!
  • Wealth: Increases your coin drops and gives you money-related abilities like Bribe and Golden Touch!
  • Agility: Increases your dodge percentage and gives you abilities like Quick Step and Shadow Strike!
  • Composure: Increases your maximum HP by +5 and gives you defensive abilities such as Iron Will and Last Stand!
