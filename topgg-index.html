Buy, upgrade, and master abilities from four categories (Strength, Wealth, Agility, Composure)!

⬆️

Ability Upgrades

Upgrade abilities up to level 15 using attribute points for massive power boosts!

💪

Customizable Stats

Distribute points to Strength, Wealth, Agility, and Composure to build your perfect hero!

🎮 Essential Commands

  • /profile create – Create your character and get 100 free points!
  • /profile distribute – Spend points to boost your stats!
  • /profile view – View your current character stats!
  • /boss fight [boss] – Battle a boss for coins!
  • /boss inspect [boss] – View boss details before fighting!
  • /abilities buy [ability] – Purchase new abilities!
  • /abilities upgrade [ability] – Upgrade your abilities (up to level 15)!
  • /abilities upgrade view [ability] – Check your upgrade progress!
