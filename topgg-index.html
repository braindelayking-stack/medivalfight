🎮 The Game

⚔️ Turn-Based Boss Battles

15 epic bosses across 4 difficulty tiers (Easy, Mid, Strong, Very Strong). From Golems to the Void Destroyer, each boss has unique attacks and customizable coin drops!

  • Easy: Golem, Skeleton, Slime, Goblin
  • Mid: Orc, Wolf, Spider, Bandit
  • Strong: Dragon, Demon, Giant, Vampire
  • Very Strong: Lich, Void Destroyer

📜 16 Powerful Abilities

Unlock and upgrade abilities across 4 categories:

  • Strength: Berserk, Smash, Power Strike, Devastate
  • Wealth: Bribe, Golden Touch, Lucky Coin, Treasure Hunter
  • Agility: Quick Step, Shadow Strike, Dodge Master, Flurry
  • Composure: Iron Will, Last Stand, Heal, Fortify

Upgrade each ability up to level 15 using your points!

💬 Yappin' Points System

Earn points just by chatting! Here's how it works:

  • Longer messages = more points
  • Admins can configure which channels give points via the dashboard
  • Use points to increase your core attributes

📈 Attribute Breakdown

  • Strength: Boosts raw damage output
  • Wealth: Increases coin drops from bosses
  • Agility: Improves dodge chance
  • Composure: +5 HP per point

🎒 More Features

  • Quests: Daily, weekly, and repeatable quests with rewards
  • Achievements: Long-term goals with exclusive titles and items
  • Inventory: Collect potions, boosters, and passive items
  • Shops: Spend coins on items or special Discord roles
  • Leaderboards: Compete for the top spot in your server
