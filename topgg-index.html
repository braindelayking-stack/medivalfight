📖 How It All Works

⚔️ Battle System

All fights against bosses will be turn-based battles of strength! Take turns hitting the enemy with your attacks! Use your abilities to get the advantage! Bosses have their own special attacks which happen at random intervals!

📜 Ability System

Unlock 16 abilities categorized into four groups: Strength, Wealth, Agility, and Composure. Abilities can be bought using either coins received for defeating bosses or special attributes' points!

Abilities aren't just commands but your arsenal! Use /fire-attack, /berserk, /shadow-strike, and other abilities in your battle!

⬆️ Ability Upgrades

Here comes the true potential of the game! Every single ability that you have can be upgraded to level 15! The cost of each upgrade is attribute points with every next upgrade being more expensive by 30 points. For example, from 1 to 2 levels you'll need 30 points, from 2 to 3 - 60 points and so on!

Upgrades make your abilities much stronger: more damage, healing, buffing, more effective — you will have lots and lots of goals to achieve in this system!

💪 Stat Point System

  • Strength: Boosts your damage output in combat
  • Wealth: Get more coins after winning against bosses, grants wealth-based abilities
  • Agility: Improves your dodging of boss attacks
  • Composure: Enhances your maximum HP (Base HP = 25, +5 HP per point)

🪙 Coin System

Earn coins by beating bosses! Your Wealth stat gives you more coins. Spend coins to get new abilities or items from shops!
