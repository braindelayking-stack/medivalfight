💪 Attribute Breakdown

  • Strength: Increases raw damage output.
  • Wealth: Boosts coin rewards and unlocks special abilities.
  • Agility: Enhances your chance to dodge incoming attacks.
  • Composure: Increases your maximum HP (+5 HP per point).
