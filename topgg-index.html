📖 How It All Works

⚔️ Battle System

Every boss fight is a turn-based clash of might! You and the boss take turns attacking. Use your abilities to turn the tide! Bosses have unique special attacks that trigger periodically—stay vigilant!

📜 Ability System

Unlock 16 powerful abilities divided into 4 categories: Strength, Wealth, Agility, and Composure. Each ability can be purchased either with coins earned from boss battles or with specific stat points—choose your path carefully!

Abilities are more than just commands—they are your weapons! Use /fire-attack, /berserk, /shadow-strike, and many more during battles to devastate your foes!

⬆️ Ability Upgrades

This is where the true depth lies! Every ability you own can be upgraded up to level 15! Each upgrade costs attribute points, with the cost increasing by 30 points per level. For example, upgrading from level 1 to 2 costs 30 points, level 2 to 3 costs 60, and so on!

Upgrades drastically enhance your abilities: more damage, higher healing, better buffs, more reliable effects—this system gives you endless goals to work towards, keeping you engaged for hours on end!

💪 Stat Point System

Your profile starts with 100 free points to distribute!

  • Strength: Increases your damage dealt in battle
  • Wealth: Earn more coins from boss victories, unlocks wealth-based abilities
  • Agility: Higher chance to dodge boss attacks
  • Composure: Boosts your maximum HP (Base HP is 25, +5 HP per point)

🪙 Coin System

Coins are earned by defeating bosses! Your Wealth stat increases your coin earnings. Use coins to buy new abilities or shop items!
