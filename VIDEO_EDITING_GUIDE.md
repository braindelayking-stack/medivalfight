
# Medieval Fight Bot Trailer Editing Guide (Using CapCut - Free!)

We're going to use **CapCut** (free for macOS/iOS/Android) - it's perfect for this! Download it here: https://www.capcut.com/

---

## 1. Remove Watermark (Most Important!)
- Open CapCut and import `medieval_fight_bot_trailer.mp4`
- If there's a watermark in the corner:
  - Go to "Cutout" → "Remove Watermark" and brush over the watermark area
  - OR zoom the video slightly (105-110%) to crop out the watermark

---

## 2. Mix in Real Discord UI
- First, record a 3-second clip of your actual Discord bot in action on your phone/mac:
  - Open Discord, go to a server with the bot
  - Hit record (use the Mac screen recorder from earlier)
  - Tap/click `/profile` or `/boss fight` to show the real UI!
- Add this 3-second clip to your trailer - replace one of the AI-generated UI shots!
- Add a subtle "screen shake" or "pop in" effect to make it fit!

---

## 3. Sharpen the Hook (Lead with Action!)
- Move your most epic clip (dragon defeated, legendary item drop) to the BEGINNING (first 2 seconds!)
- Cut the original opening question - move it to right after the epic action clip!

---

## 4. Tighten the Middle (Trim the Fat!)
- Watch the video from 15-20 seconds - if it feels slow, cut those frames!
- Keep the pacing tight - every second should be exciting!

---

## 5. Add Sound Design
- CapCut has a huge free sound effects library! Add:
  - Sword clinks when bosses are fighting
  - Level-up chimes when showing stats
  - Discord notification pings when showing chat
- Keep the sound effects subtle - don't overpower the voiceover!
- Make sure your background music is epic fantasy royalty-free (CapCut has lots!)

---

## 6. Conversion Optimization
- **Add a CTA overlay**:
  - For TikTok/Reels: Add a text sticker that says "LINK IN BIO →" with an arrow pointing up
  - For YouTube Shorts: Add "CHECK PINNED COMMENT!" in bold text at the bottom
- **Make a perfect loop**:
  - Watch the first and last frames
  - Edit the last frame to fade/transition smoothly back to the first frame - so it loops perfectly when watched on repeat!

---

## 7. Export & Post!
- Export in 1080p, 9:16 aspect ratio (1080x1920)
- Post to TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts!
- Use the hashtags: #DiscordBot #Discord #Gaming #Medieval #Fantasy #RPG #Viral #NewGame #AI

Good luck - your trailer is gonna be amazing! 🚀
